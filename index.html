Why 80%?

Your battery is happiest below full.

Phone batteries wear a little with every charge, and they wear faster when they sit at 100% for hours, especially when they are warm. Stopping around 80% most days is one of the easiest ways to keep yours healthy for longer.

It is the same idea behind the charge limits phone makers now build in. My Battery makes the habit easy on any phone, and leaves you free to charge to 100% when you need it.

Good habits, in short

  • Unplug around 80% on most days
  • Keep it cool while charging: no pillows, no sun
  • Charge to 100% when a long day needs it; that's fine
  • Overnight? Let your phone's own limit hold it

How it works

Three small steps.

  1. Plug in

    My Battery starts watching by itself, with a quiet note while it charges and how long until your limit.

  2. Get a gentle nudge

    At your limit you hear your chosen chime. Snooze it, or charge to 100% this time.

  3. Unplug

    The reminder stops the moment the cable comes out, and the charge goes into your week.

Your phone can help too

Many phones can stop at 80% themselves.

Android does not let apps switch charging off, so My Battery reminds you instead. Many phones have a limit built in. Turn it on if yours does: your phone then holds at 80%, and My Battery simply tells you there is nothing to do.

Menu names change between phones and versions. My Battery shows the steps for your phone under Settings > Background watching.

Where to look

  • Google Pixel: Settings > Battery > Charging optimization, then Limit to 80%.
  • Samsung Galaxy: Settings > Battery > Battery protection (or Protect battery), then Maximum 80%.
  • OnePlus, Oppo, Realme: Settings > Battery > Battery health or Optimized charging. Some models can stop at 80%.
  • Xiaomi, Redmi, Poco: Settings > Battery > look for Optimized charging or Smart charging.
  • Motorola: Settings > Battery > Optimized charging or Overcharge protection.

FAQ

Good questions.

Can My Battery stop charging at 80%?

No app can: Android does not let apps switch charging off. My Battery watches the level and reminds you to unplug. Many phones can stop at 80% by themselves; here is where to look. If yours does, My Battery sees it holding and stays quiet.

Why is there a note while my phone charges?

Android asks any app that keeps running to show a note. Ours is quiet, shows the level and the time to your limit, and goes away when you unplug.

Why does it ask to run in the background?

So Android lets My Battery start watching by itself when you plug in. Most phones call this "Unrestricted" battery use; OnePlus, OPPO and realme call it "Allow background activity". Without it, Android may pause the app; you then get a note saying "Tap to watch this charge", and one tap starts it. My Battery only runs while your phone is plugged in.

Will it use much battery?

It runs only while your phone is plugged in and charging, and stops when you unplug. When nothing is charging it does nothing at all, unless you turn on the low battery nudge, which takes a quick look every half hour.

Is 80% right for me?

80% is a good everyday choice. If you need more, pick 85% or 90%. Before a long day, tap Charge to 100% once; the next charge goes back to your limit.

Does it work with wireless charging?

Yes: wired, USB, wireless and docks all count.

Is My Battery free?

Yes. There are no ads, no subscriptions and nothing to buy.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 8.0 (Oreo) and newer.

How do I delete everything?

History > Clear history deletes your charges, and uninstalling deletes the rest. There is nothing to delete anywhere else.

How do I get help or share an idea?

In the app, open Settings > About My Battery > Send feedback, or write to hello@onelifestack.com.
