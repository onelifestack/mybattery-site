Press kit

My Battery is a free, private Android app that nudges you to unplug at 80%. For anything else, write to hello@onelifestack.com.

In one line

My Battery watches while your phone charges and gives you a gentle nudge at your limit, so your battery lasts longer; everything stays on your phone.

Facts

  • Name: My Battery
  • Maker: OneLifeStack
  • Platform: Android 8.0 and newer
  • Price: free, no ads, no purchases
  • Privacy: no account, no analytics, no internet permission
